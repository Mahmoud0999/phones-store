📋 Return Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority

Our Return & Exchange Policy

30-Day Money Back Guarantee

We offer a full 30-day money-back guarantee on all phones purchased from Sharkawy. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply return it within 30 days of purchase for a full refund or exchange.

Condition Requirements

  • Device must be in original, unused condition
  • All original packaging, cables, and accessories must be included
  • No visible signs of wear or damage
  • Screen protector must be intact
  • Original box and documentation required

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact our customer service team at support@sharkawy.com
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Receive a prepaid return shipping label
  4. Pack the item securely with all original components
  5. Ship the package using the provided label
  6. Receive refund within 5-7 business days of receipt

Defective Products

If your phone arrives with a defect, we'll provide a replacement or full refund at no cost. We cover:

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Dead batteries
  • Hardware malfunctions
  • Screen issues (not caused by physical damage)

2-Year Warranty Coverage

All Sharkawy phones come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty that covers:

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Hardware failures
  • Free repair or replacement
  • Accidental damage protection (additional premium available)

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Phones damaged by user mishandling
  • Water-damaged devices
  • Phones with cracked screens caused by drops
  • Devices returned after 30 days
  • Items purchased from third-party sellers

Shipping Information

  • Return shipping is FREE on all valid returns
  • We cover the cost of return labels
  • Use track & trace for your return shipment
  • Insurance is included on all returns
